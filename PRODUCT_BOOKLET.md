## SECTION 1: WHAT IS IDEAL ICP?

Ideal ICP is an AI-powered advertising diagnostic platform that tells B2B marketers, founders, and growth teams exactly why their ads are underperforming — and what to fix next.

Most businesses waste between 30% and 60% of their monthly ad budget targeting people who will never buy. Not because their product is wrong. Because their targeting is wrong. Ideal ICP finds the gap between who you are targeting and who actually buys from you — and scores it.

The result: a living ICP Health Score between 0 and 100, a ranked list of findings, and a prioritised fix plan — all reviewed by a practitioner on our B2B media buying team.

**ICP stands for Ideal Customer Profile.** It is the detailed description of the type of company or person most likely to buy your product, get value from it, and stay a customer. When your ads target anyone other than your ICP, you pay for clicks that never convert.

## SECTION 4: HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

### The Three Entry Points

**Entry Point 1: The Questionnaire**
The standard path. Answer 22 questions across three layers. Takes 5 minutes. No ad account access required. Instant results.

**Entry Point 2: CSV Upload (Campaign Data)**
For users who have no time for the questionnaire. Drop a Google Ads or Meta CSV export directly on the homepage. The system analyses the file after sign-in and shows results immediately on the dashboard.

**Entry Point 3: Existing Dashboard**

---

### The Full Diagnostic Flow

**Step 1 — Answer 22 Questions**
The questionnaire is divided into three layers. Each layer unlocks the next. Questions adapt based on whether you are B2B or B2C. Answers are saved automatically. A progress bar and XP system (10 XP per question) keeps users engaged.

**Step 2 — The AI Generates Your Report**
After the final submission, the system:
- Saves all answers securely
- Visits your landing page URL and analyses it
- Researches industry CPC and CPA benchmarks for your region
- Maps your competitive landscape
- Generates your diagnostic report

This process takes approximately 30–60 seconds and is shown to the user via a live progress indicator.

**Step 3 — Your ICP Health Score**
A score between 0 and 100 appears, with breakdowns across:
- Audience alignment
- Landing page fit
- Budget efficiency
- Competitive position

**Step 4 — Ranked Findings**
Every issue found is classified as Critical, Warning, or Opportunity, with a full explanation and specific fix instructions.

**Step 5 — Quick Wins Action Plan**
Three to five specific, ranked actions you can implement this week to improve your score. Each win can be marked complete in the dashboard, which tracks your streak.

**Step 6 — Media Buyer Review**
Every report is reviewed by a practitioner from the Ideal ICP media buying team. If they identify a critical targeting gap, they follow up personally within 24 hours. Included on the free tier.

## SECTION 5: THE QUESTIONNAIRE — 22 QUESTIONS ACROSS 3 LAYERS

### Layer 1: ICP Foundation
*Establishing who your best customers are and what drives them.*

1. What does your business do? Describe your core product or service.
2. What industry or vertical do you operate in?
3. How does your business primarily make sales? (B2B, E-commerce, B2C Services, SaaS, Local, Marketplace)
4. What is the typical company size of your best customers? (B2B only)
   — OR — Describe your ideal customer as a person (B2C only)
5. What core problem or desire did your best customers have before working with you?
6. How did your best customers typically discover you?
7. What is your average deal size per client? (B2B only)
   — OR — What is your average order value? (B2C only)
8. What job titles hold the final buying decision? (B2B only)
   — OR — What typically triggers a purchase decision? (B2C only)

### Layer 2: Targeting Mismatch
*Comparing your ideal customer against who you are actually reaching.*

9. Describe who you currently believe your ideal customer is.
10. Which ad channels are you currently running? (Meta, Google Search, Google Display, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Twitter/X, Programmatic, Retargeting, Organic only)
11. What is the URL of your main landing page?
12. What is the primary geographic region you are targeting?
13. Describe your current ad targeting setup — audiences, interests, or keywords.
14. What is your total monthly ad spend?
15. How many paying customers or qualified leads did you acquire in the last 3 months?
16. Do the people responding to your ads match the profile of your best customers?
17. What percentage of interested prospects convert into paying customers?
18. What is the average lifetime value of a paying customer?

### Layer 3: Funnel Friction
*Auditing your landing page and conversion path for friction points.*

19. What is the primary call-to-action on your main landing or product page?
20. What must a customer do or provide before completing a purchase or booking?
21. How easy is your landing or product page to use on a mobile device? (1–10 slider)
22. What trust signals do you currently show on your landing page?
23. How clearly does your page communicate why you are different from alternatives? (1–10 slider)

## SECTION 6: THE ICP HEALTH SCORE

The ICP Health Score is a number between 0 and 100 that represents how closely aligned your advertising is with your real buyers.

**Score Ranges:**
- 80–100 — Strong alignment. Minor optimisations available.
- 60–79 — Moderate misalignment. Meaningful budget waste present.
- 40–59 — Significant misalignment. High probability of poor-fit leads.
- Below 40 — Severe misalignment. Budget is largely wasted on non-buyers.

**What goes into the score:**
The score is calculated across six weighted dimensions:
1. Audience alignment — How well your current targeting matches your actual ICP
2. Landing page fit — Does your page speak to your ICP's pain points and intent?
3. Budget efficiency — Is spend allocated to channels and audiences most likely to convert?
4. Competitive position — Where you sit relative to alternatives your ICP considers
5. Funnel friction — How many unnecessary barriers exist between a click and a conversion
6. Lead quality — Whether the volume you generate reflects the quality your ICP represents

**The Score Improves Over Time**
Every time a user re-runs their diagnosis, uploads campaign CSV data, or implements a quick win, their score can change. The dashboard tracks score history across all reports.

**CSV Score Delta**
When campaign CSV data is uploaded and analysed, the system calculates a score delta — a specific number of points of potential improvement available if the recommendations from the CSV are implemented. This is stored and shown on the overview.

## SECTION 8: THE AI BEHIND THE PLATFORM

Ideal ICP is powered by Claude, Anthropic's AI model. We use different model tiers depending on the task.

### Models Used

**Claude Opus** — The highest-capability model. Used for:
- Generating full ICP diagnostic reports (all findings, scores, quick wins)
- Deep competitive intelligence with live web research
- Full campaign CSV analysis with profile context
- Weekly intelligence briefing generation

**Claude Haiku** — A fast, cost-efficient model. Used for:
- CSV file validation (checking whether an uploaded file is genuine ad data before running the expensive full analysis)
- Quick pre-checks and routing decisions

### What the AI Does in Each Feature

**ICP Diagnostic Report**
The AI visits the user's landing page URL, researches regional advertising benchmarks with live web search, analyses competitor positioning, and generates a structured JSON report covering all diagnostic dimensions. It is instructed to speak like a senior media buyer, reference real numbers, and never invent data.

**Weekly Intelligence Briefing**
Before writing a single word, the AI runs four mandatory web searches:
1. Industry advertising benchmarks (CPC, CPA) for the user's region and month
2. Named competitors in the user's market
3. Platform algorithm updates for the user's primary ad channel
4. Regional digital advertising market trends

The briefing contains five insights with real source citations, a competitor positions map, a benchmark table, and specific opportunity and recommendation statements — all personalised to the user's industry, channels, budget, and ICP score.

**CSV Analysis**
Two-step process:
1. Haiku validates whether the uploaded file is genuine advertising campaign data
2. If valid, Opus analyses up to 500 rows with the user's business profile as context, identifying top performers, underperformers, budget waste, audience insights, and three ranked recommendations — plus a score delta

**Validation (Rejection Logic)**
If a user uploads a CSV that is not advertising data (e.g. a CRM export, inventory file, or financial statement), the AI returns a clear rejection with the specific reason and what to upload instead. No full analysis runs and no cost is incurred.

### What the AI Does Not Do
- It never accesses your ad accounts directly
- It never stores your questionnaire answers outside the Ideal ICP platform
- It never fabricates benchmark numbers — all data comes from web research at the time of generation
- It does not replace your media buyer. It prepares the ground so the media buyer's time is focused on decisions, not data gathering.

### Privacy and Data Handling
- No Google OAuth. No Meta permissions. Zero.
- Users answer questions. That is the only data the AI sees.
- Stored securely in Supabase. Never shared. Never sold.
- CSV files are processed and discarded — not stored in raw form.

## SECTION 10: THE CSV ANALYSIS FEATURE

### What It Is
Users can upload a CSV export from any ad platform — Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, TikTok, LinkedIn — and receive an AI-powered campaign analysis in 30–60 seconds.

### What It Analyses
- Top 3 performing campaigns or ad sets and why they are winning
- Bottom 3 underperforming campaigns and what is going wrong
- Budget waste estimate with specific campaigns identified
- 2–4 audience behaviour insights visible in the data
- 3 ranked recommendations with estimated revenue or cost impact
- A score delta: the number of ICP Health Score points available if the recommendations are implemented

### Supported Formats
- Google Ads campaign performance exports
- Meta Ads Manager table exports
- Any CSV with campaign names, spend, and at least one performance metric
- Files with Google-style metadata headers are automatically handled

### Validation
Every file is validated by AI before the full analysis runs. Files that are not advertising data (CRM exports, financial statements, inventory files) are rejected with a clear explanation and instructions on what to upload instead. This keeps the analysis accurate and prevents wasted time.

### Email Notification
When the analysis finds a score delta of +5 or more points, an email is sent to the user with the summary, top recommendation, and a link back to the full analysis. Low-impact uploads do not trigger an email.

### The Homepage Hook
Users who have no time for the questionnaire can drop their CSV directly on the idealicp.com homepage. The file is saved temporarily, the user is taken to sign up or log in, and upon returning to the dashboard, the file is already loaded and ready to analyse — no re-upload required.

## SECTION 11: WEEKLY INTELLIGENCE BRIEFING

### What It Is
A personalised weekly market briefing sent to paid subscribers every Monday. Researched and generated specifically for the user's industry, region, and ad channels — not a generic newsletter.

### What It Contains
**Five Market Insights** — each with:
- A specific title referencing real numbers
- Two to three sentences with actual data
- The source name
- A realistic relative timestamp (e.g. "3 days ago", "Earlier this week")
- Implication for the user's campaigns (where relevant)
- A specific recommended action (where relevant)

Insight types include: market movements, competitor strategies, platform updates, and opportunities.

**Competitor Positions Map** — A 2x2 chart placing four named local competitors on Market Presence vs ICP Quality axes.

**Benchmark Table** — Six metrics compared: CTR, CPA, Landing Page Conversion Rate, Lead Quality Score, LTV:CAC Ratio, ICP Health Score. User values, industry average, and top 10% all shown.

**Top Opportunity** — One sentence identifying the single biggest opportunity in the user's market that week.

**Top Recommended Action** — One specific action to take this week.

### On-Demand Email (Paid Tiers)
Users can send the current briefing to their inbox at any time using the "Send to inbox" button in the Intelligence tab. Rate-limited to once every four hours to prevent spam. Free tier users cannot use on-demand email — it requires a paid subscription.

### Generation
Every briefing is generated fresh using Claude Opus with live web search tools. Four searches are run before any output is written. The system uses real source names, real competitor names from the user's region, and real benchmark figures — not invented data.

## SECTION 16: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**What is the difference between free and paid?**
The free report diagnoses your ICP based on your questionnaire answers and gives you your health score, top 3 findings, and quick wins. Paid tiers go further: our team visits your actual landing page, researches your competitors, and benchmarks your metrics with live regional data. Paid tiers also include monthly re-diagnosis, CSV analysis, and the weekly intelligence briefing.

**Do you need my ad account access?**
No. Never. You answer 22 questions and optionally upload a CSV export of your campaign data. That is it. No Google OAuth. No Meta permissions. No compliance headaches.

**How is this different from a marketing consultant?**
A consultant charges KES 50,000 or more for a strategy session. Ideal ICP gives you a living diagnostic that updates every month, tells you what to fix next automatically, and is backed by a team of practitioners — for a fraction of the cost.

**What markets do you cover?**
Kenya is our primary market. Tanzania, Uganda, Nigeria, South Africa, UK, and US are coming soon. Every recommendation reflects local ad costs and regional audience behaviour.

**How accurate is the waste estimate?**
The waste estimate is derived from your ICP score, budget size, industry benchmarks, and the specific misalignments found in your diagnostic. It represents the estimated portion of your monthly ad spend reaching audiences with a low probability of converting.

**Is there really a free diagnosis?**
Yes. One lifetime diagnosis at no cost, no credit card required. You get your ICP health score, top 3 findings, a quick wins plan, and a personal review from a member of our B2B media buying team.

**Does a real person review my report?**
Yes. Every diagnosis is reviewed by a practitioner on our B2B media buying team. If they identify a critical targeting gap, they follow up directly with a personal note within 24 hours. This is included on the free tier.

**Can I use this for multiple clients?**
Yes. The Agency tier is designed for teams managing multiple accounts. It includes white-label reports, multi-client coordination, and a dedicated account manager.

**How does the CSV upload work?**
Export your campaign data from Google Ads or Meta Ads Manager as a CSV file. Upload it on the dashboard or directly on the homepage. The AI validates the file, analyses up to 500 rows, and returns findings in under 60 seconds. No ad account access is required at any point.

**How often should I re-run my diagnosis?**
Monthly. Starter users get monthly monitoring included. The dashboard shows a nudge when your report is more than 30 days old. Pro and Agency users with fast-moving campaigns may benefit from re-running every two weeks.
