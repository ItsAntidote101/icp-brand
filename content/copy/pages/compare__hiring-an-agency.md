## split
### What an agency does that Ideal ICP does not
- Runs the ads for you, inside your account.
- Makes the photos, videos and designs.

That is real work and worth paying for. Ideal ICP hands you files and wording. You still press the buttons yourself.

## ladder
### Side by side
Six plain differences. A seventh: Ideal ICP is new, built on campaigns its founder ran in the United States, the United Kingdom and South Africa.
#### Who runs the ads
An agency does. With Ideal ICP you do, using our files, with your media buyer alongside.
#### Photos and video
An agency can make them for you. We do not.
#### Where the checking starts
We start after the click: who is asking, how fast you reply, whether anyone follows up.
#### Access to your account
An agency needs it to do its job. Our diagnosis needs ten answers and no login.
#### The monthly fee
Fees published on the Nairobi agency sites we read in November 2026 started at about KES 35,000 a month before ad spend, and our plans run from KES 6,500 to KES 26,000 a month.
#### Commitment
With Ideal ICP you pay month by month, by M-Pesa or card, and nothing renews on its own. Ask any agency for its terms in writing.

## split
### Pick the agency when
- Your inquiries already turn into buyers and you want more of them.
- Nobody in the business has time to open the ad account.
- You need photos, video and design made for you.

If the inquiries are not buying yet, find out why first. You will brief the agency better, or find you do not need one.

## faq
### Two questions owners ask
#### Can I use Ideal ICP while an agency runs my ads?
Yes. The diagnosis needs no login, and you can pass the files and wording to whoever runs your ads.
#### Will you manage my ads if I ask?
Not in the plans. From the dashboard, ask your media buyer about full management.
