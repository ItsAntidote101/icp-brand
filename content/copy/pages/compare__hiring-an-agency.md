## split
### What an agency does that I do not
- Runs the ads for you, inside your account.
- Makes the creative: the photos, the videos, the designs.

That is real work and worth paying for. Ideal ICP hands you files and wording. You still press the buttons yourself.

## ladder
### Side by side
Six plain differences.
#### Who runs the ads
An agency does. With Ideal ICP you do, using my files, with me alongside.
#### Photos and video
An agency can make them for you. I do not.
#### Where the checking starts
I start after the click: who is asking, how fast you reply and whether anyone follows up.
#### Access to your account
An agency needs it to do its job. My diagnosis needs ten answers and no login.
#### The monthly fee
Fees published by Nairobi agencies I read in November 2026 started at about KES 35,000 a month before ad spend, and my plans run from KES 6,500 to KES 26,000 a month.
#### Commitment
With me you pay one month at a time, by M-Pesa or card, and nothing renews on its own. Ask any agency for its terms in writing.

## split
### Pick the agency when
- Your inquiries already turn into buyers and you want more of them.
- Nobody in the business has time to open the ad account.
- You need photos, video and design made for you.

If the inquiries are not buying yet, find out why first. You will brief the agency better, or find you do not need one.
