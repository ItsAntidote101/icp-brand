A long voice note about a land dispute gets a long, careful answer. They get what they came for, and the consultation fee never comes up.

"How much to register a company?" could come from a founder ready to pay today or a student with an assignment. Without two questions up front, both get your afternoon.

## faq
### Questions firms ask
#### Are advocates allowed to advertise this way?
Advocates work under advertising rules, and you should check the current ones before any ad runs. You paste every word yourself, so nothing goes out unread, and none of our wording promises an outcome in a matter.
#### How does a firm charge a consultation fee without scaring people off?
Name it in the first reply, say what the session covers, and ask one question about the matter. Some will leave. That is the reply doing its job.
#### Is this page for accountants and consultants too?
It is. Swap consultation fee for proposal and brief for scope. The loss is the same: work done before anyone agrees to pay.
#### Do you run the firm's ads?
We do not. You are shown what to fix and handed the files and wording, and on a paid plan your cost per inquiry is tracked each week. For full management, ask by message.

## notes
Before this page goes live, check the current advertising rules for advocates (Advocates Act and the Law Society of Kenya's practice rules) against every line here and against any ad or reply wording handed to a law firm. The research file's only source is a 2016 article reporting limits such as no promised outcomes, and it says to check current rules. Nobody has checked them.
Accountants may have their own advertising rules through their regulator. Not checked either.
The page promises no outcome for the firm or for any client matter. Keep it that way in edits.
Hero scene (forty minutes explaining succession law by phone to someone who then asks for "a small discount") is the professional services scene in research/kenya-and-demand.md part 2. The research presents part 2 as typical phrasing from general knowledge, so no real caller said this. "Forty minutes" is an illustration, not a measured figure.
Terms borrowed from that entry: consultation fee, retainer, fee note, brief, free advice, engagement letter, the land dispute voice note, "How much to register a company?".
Meta customer file: offered as something the firm can skip, because client lists are confidential. This is the copywriter's caution, not a Meta rule. Decide whether to remove the file from this trade altogether.
