# What should a lead cost in Kenya?
There is no single right cost per lead in Kenya. The number that matters is the most you can afford to pay for one, and you can work it out in two minutes from your own sales.

## prose
### What is the two minute sum?

You need three numbers from your own business.

- **Profit per sale.** What you keep from one customer after your costs, not the selling price.
- **How many leads become customers.** Count last month's leads and last month's sales.
- **How much of that profit you will spend to win the customer.** That is your decision. If you are unsure, start at a third and adjust.

Multiply them together. That is the most a lead is worth to you.

Say you keep KES 40,000 from each sale. One lead in twenty buys, so each lead is worth KES 2,000 of profit on average. If you will spend up to 30% of that to win the customer, the most you should pay for a lead is **KES 600**. The figures are an example. Use your own.

### Why can a cheap lead be the expensive one?

A campaign that brings leads at KES 150 each looks better than one at KES 500. But say one in fifty of the cheap leads buys and one in ten of the dearer leads buys. The cheap campaign costs KES 7,500 for each customer and the dearer one costs KES 5,000.

Always carry the sum through to cost per customer before you decide which campaign is working. To see why cheap forms fill up with people who never buy, read [LINK: why Facebook leads do not buy -> /guides/why-facebook-leads-dont-buy].

### What if I do not know my numbers?

Many owners do not, and that is the first thing to fix. Start a weekly habit: write down what you spent on ads, how many leads came in and how many sales closed. After four weeks you can do the sum above with your own figures. The weekly check is laid out in [LINK: how to know if your ads are working -> /guides/how-to-know-if-ads-are-working].

### What should I check when my cost per lead goes up?

Three things, before you panic. Did you change the audience? Has the same ad been running for weeks, so people are tired of it? Did the leads get better? A dearer lead that buys more often is good news.

## faq
### Short answers
#### Is there an average cost per lead for Kenya?
Figures you find online are averages across other businesses, often in other countries, selling other things. Your own profit per sale decides what you can pay.
#### Should I pause a campaign with a high cost per lead?
Only after you have carried the sum through to cost per customer. A dearer lead can be the cheaper customer.
#### How long before the numbers mean anything?
Give it enough leads to count buyers, not a fixed number of days. A handful of leads tells you very little either way.
