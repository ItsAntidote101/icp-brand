# Boost post or Ads Manager: which one should I use?
A boost buys attention for a post you already published. Ads Manager lets you ask for inquiries and say who should see the ad, so use it when you want people to call, chat or fill a form.

## prose
### What does the Boost button really do?
It turns a post you already published into an ad. Meta is plain about this on its help page: a boosted post needs a budget, and it shows on your bill as an ad.

When you boost, you decide five things: a goal, the text, the schedule, the budget and where the post appears. The audience is picked by age, gender, location and interests.

It takes two minutes, which is the whole appeal.

### Why did my boost bring likes and no customers?
Because of the goal. Meta looks for the people most likely to do the thing you asked for. Ask for engagement and it finds people who like and comment. They are real people. Liking is simply what they do.

Meta's own page says boosting fits when you want more engagement on your Page or more awareness of your name. Two hundred likes and no deposit is the boost doing what it was told.

Run this sum on your last boost. Say KES 3,000 brought 200 likes and 2 messages, and neither message turned into a sale. Counted by likes, that is KES 15 each and looks cheap. Counted by messages, it is KES 1,500 each. Counted by buyers, it is KES 3,000 for nothing. Those numbers are made up to show the arithmetic. They are not a benchmark.

### How do I check what my last boost was asking for?
Open your Facebook Page and go to Ad Center. Find the boosted post and click View results. Meta names the ad after the goal you chose, so a boost that ran for engagement is titled "Post engagements". If that is what you see, you paid for likes and got them.

### Can a boost bring inquiries at all?
Yes, if you change the goal before you pay. The boost screen on Facebook has a goal called "Get more messages", and you can point it at WhatsApp. For that you need a WhatsApp Business number connected to your Page. Meta offers this for posts with a single photo, a video or a link.

That is the cheapest fix on this page. Same post, same budget, a different request.

### What does Ads Manager add?
Three things that matter when you want inquiries:
- An objective called Leads. Ads Manager has six objectives (Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App promotion, Sales), and Leads is the one built to collect inquiries by message, phone call or sign-up.
- Instant forms, which open inside Facebook or Instagram with no website involved. The "Higher intent" form type adds a review screen, so people check their details before they send.
- More say over who sees the ad. You can upload your own customer list as a custom audience, then ask Meta for a lookalike audience of people who resemble it. Two limits to know: Meta does not allow health or financial information in those lists, and lookalike audiences are restricted for some housing and finance ads.

The screen has far more switches than the Boost button. That is the price of being able to choose. If you want the choices laid out for your stage, the Pro plan includes a campaign setup plan that I review myself.

### So which one should I use this week?
- You are telling people who already know you about a new branch, an open day or new hours: boost it.
- You want calls, chats or forms from people who have never heard of you: use Ads Manager with the Leads objective, or at the least a boost with the goal set to messages.
- Either way, decide who replies and how fast before you pay. An inquiry that waits two days was wasted whichever button you pressed.

If the inquiries are already coming and nobody buys, the button is not your problem.

## faq
### Questions owners ask next
#### Is a boosted post cheaper than an Ads Manager ad?
Neither has a fixed price. You set the budget in both. What changes is what Meta tries to get you for the money.
#### Do I need a website to use Ads Manager?
No. An instant form opens inside Facebook or Instagram, and an ad that clicks to WhatsApp opens a chat with your business number.
