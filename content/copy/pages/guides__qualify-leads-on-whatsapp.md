# How do I qualify leads on WhatsApp?
Ask three things before you quote: what they need, when they need it, and what budget they have in mind. Serious buyers answer. Browsers go quiet, and you find out in two minutes instead of two weeks.

## prose
### What are the three questions?

- **What exactly are you looking for?** "Is it the 2 bedroom or the 3 bedroom you are interested in?"
- **When do you need it?** "Are you looking to move this month, or planning ahead?"
- **What budget are you working with?** "So I show you the right options, what range did you have in mind?"

Ask them one at a time, in a friendly way, before you send a price list. It may feel like you are being difficult, but the questions save their time as well as yours.

### How do I reply fast and keep track?

The WhatsApp Business app lets you set a greeting that goes out automatically, save your common answers as quick replies, and tag chats with labels. Use all three.

After the questions, sort every new chat into one of three labels.

- **Ready now.** Knows what they want, has the budget, wants it soon. Call them today.
- **Later.** Interested but not this month. Follow up on a date you agree.
- **Not a fit.** Thank them and move on.

### When should I give the price?

Early. Hiding the price until the end only keeps the wrong people in the chat longer. A range such as "from KES 4.5M, with a payment plan" lets people sort themselves. The figure is a placeholder. Use your own. Wording for the "How much?" moment is in [LINK: why people ask for last price and disappear -> /guides/last-price-and-disappear].

### How many times should I follow up?

Three, then stop. Most "let me get back to you" chats need a nudge. Follow up the next day, three days later and a week later, each time with something useful such as a new photo, a date that is filling up or an answer to a question they asked. After the third, label the chat Later and let it go.

### What should I stop doing?

Sending the full price list or brochure to everyone as your first reply. It ends the conversation before you have learned anything about the buyer.

## faq
### Short answers
#### Will asking questions scare buyers away?
It puts off people who were only browsing. Someone who wants to buy is glad to be asked what they need.
#### Should the questions go in the ad form or in the chat?
Either works, as long as someone reads the answers. Whether to send people to WhatsApp or to a form is covered in [LINK: click to WhatsApp ads or lead forms -> /guides/click-to-whatsapp-or-lead-forms].
#### What if several people answer the business phone?
Save the three questions as quick replies and agree the three labels. Then everyone sorts chats the same way.
