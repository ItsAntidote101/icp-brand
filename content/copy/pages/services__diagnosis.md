## cards
### What it asks
#### What you sell and where you advertise
The business, and where your ads run.
#### Your numbers, roughly
What goes into ads in a month, how many inquiries that brings, how many buy and what one sale is worth.
#### Who is asking
Whether the people who inquire look like your best customers.
[LINK: How this looks in property -> /for/property]
#### How fast they hear back
How quickly a new inquiry gets a reply.

## cards
### What comes back
#### Your own arithmetic
Your cost per inquiry, your cost per sale, and what one more buyer in every ten inquiries would be worth. All of it comes from the figures you gave.
#### A score out of 100
Or a launch plan, if you have not started ads.
#### Your top problems
In plain language.
#### Three fixes for this week
With the words to use for the first one. Wording for every fix comes with Starter.
[LINK: What the paid plans add -> /pricing]
#### A call, if you ask for it
One free 20 minute call with me. We open your report and agree what to fix first.
#### A healthy verdict, if you earned one
If most of your inquiries buy, the report says so and shows what to protect before you spend more.

## cards
### What it does not do
#### Look inside your ad account
It works from your answers, so it is only as good as what you tell it. It cannot see single campaigns, keywords or ads.
#### Borrow other people's numbers
Your cost per inquiry and cost per sale are plain arithmetic on your own figures.
#### Change anything in your ads
It tells you what to fix. Nothing in your account moves unless you move it.
#### Put you on a call list
Nobody phones. The call happens only if you ask.

## cards

## faq
### Questions about the diagnosis
#### Is it really free?
Yes. One diagnosis and one 20 minute call, with no card. You pay only if you later choose a plan.
#### What if I do not know my exact numbers?
Rough numbers from memory are enough. You pick a range for your ad spend and give your best estimate of inquiries.
#### What if I have not started ads?
Say so at the first question. You get a launch plan instead of a score.
#### What is the difference between the free report and the full one?
The free one asks 10 questions and shows your top problems. The full report, on Starter, asks about 10 more on your best customer and your follow up, and checks your landing page if your ads use one. You get every problem, with the whole fix plan.
