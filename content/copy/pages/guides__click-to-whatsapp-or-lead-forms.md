# Click to WhatsApp ads or lead forms?
WhatsApp ads bring faster chats and forms bring details you can call back. Both fail without a plan for who replies, how fast and with what words.
[IMAGE: A phone showing a new WhatsApp chat that came from an ad, beside a laptop with a downloaded sheet of form entries | 16:9]

## prose
### What is the difference?

An ad that clicks to WhatsApp has a button that opens a chat with your business number. The person lands in a conversation, and you have to hold up your end of it. To run one you need a WhatsApp Business number connected to your Facebook Page.

A lead form, which Meta calls an instant form, opens inside Facebook or Instagram. Meta fills in the name and phone number from the person's profile, so sending it takes almost no effort. You receive a row of details, and the conversation is still yours to start.

### Which one suits my business?

Look at how your sales happen.

Choose WhatsApp when people ask a question or two and then decide: a facial, a dental check, a short course, a house viewing this Saturday. It only works if somebody can answer within minutes while the ad is running. A chat left until morning belongs to a person who has had all night to ask someone else.

Choose forms when a phone call is how you sell, or when inquiries arrive at hours nobody can chat. Insurance quotes, school admissions and legal consultations often fit here. The form will wait for you. The person will not wait long. A form filled at 11pm and called back two days later is how this goes wrong.

If you cannot decide, count last month's buyers. Note how many started on WhatsApp and how many started with a call. Go where the buyers were.

### How do I set up WhatsApp ads so the chats are worth having?

- In Ads Manager, pick the Leads objective, then select WhatsApp in the Conversion section. Meta sets the performance goal to "Maximize number of conversations". That goal counts chats started, whether or not anyone buys.
- In the message template section, you can suggest questions for people to tap. Write one that sorts them, such as "What does the 3 bedroom cost, and is there a payment plan?"
- In the WhatsApp Business app, save your first replies as quick replies so whoever holds the phone can answer in seconds.
- Turn on an away message for the hours nobody is on the phone. Set its schedule to "Outside of business hours", which needs your hours filled in on the business profile, and say in the message when you will reply.

### How do I set up a form so the entries are real?

- Pick the "Higher intent" form type. It adds a review screen where the person confirms their details before sending. The default type, "More volume", has no such screen.
- Add one or two custom questions the person must answer themselves, as multiple choice or a short answer. "When do you want to start?" works. So does "Which branch is nearest to you?"
- Download the entries often, from Ads Manager or from Instant forms in Meta Business Suite. Meta keeps each entry available for 90 days from the day it was sent, and after that you cannot download it.

### What reply plan does either one need?

Write down three decisions before the ad goes live.

- Who answers. One name, and a second name for their day off.
- How fast. Minutes during open hours. After hours, an away message that gives a time, then a reply first thing.
- What they say. A first reply that answers the question and asks one back, plus a follow up for the next day.

The wording is the easiest part to leave to chance. The questions to ask are in [LINK: how to qualify inquiries on WhatsApp -> /guides/qualify-leads-on-whatsapp]. From the Starter plan, Ideal ICP hands you [LINK: the words to paste for every fix -> /services/ready-to-upload], including replies and form questions.

### Can I run both?

Yes, as two separate campaigns, so each gets its own weekly line of spend, inquiries and buyers. After a month, compare what one buyer cost, and ignore what one inquiry cost.

Here is the sum with made-up numbers. KES 12,000 on WhatsApp ads brings 60 chats and 3 buyers: KES 200 a chat, KES 4,000 a buyer. KES 12,000 on forms brings 24 entries and 4 buyers: KES 500 an entry, KES 3,000 a buyer. The dearer inquiry was the cheaper buyer. Yours may fall the other way, which is why you count.

## faq
### Short answers
#### Do ads that click to WhatsApp need a website?
No. The button opens a chat with your business number. You need a WhatsApp Business number connected to your Facebook Page.
#### Why do people who filled my form not remember doing it?
The form filled in their details for them, so some sent it without much thought. Use the "Higher intent" type, add a question they must answer themselves and call sooner.
#### Can Meta aim for better inquiries, and not only more of them?
There is a performance goal called "Maximize number of qualified leads". Meta's help page says new campaigns need a Conversions API connection to use it, which means sending Meta your sales records first.
