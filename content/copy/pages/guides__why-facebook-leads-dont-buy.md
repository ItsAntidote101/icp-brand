# Why are my Facebook leads not buying?
The ad reached people who were curious, not people who were ready, and nothing between the ad and the sale told them apart. It is usually a targeting and follow-up problem, and a bigger budget will not fix it.

## prose
### Is the form too easy to fill in?

Facebook lead forms fill in a person's name and number for them. Two taps and they count as a lead, whether or not they want what you sell, and often they have forgotten your ad an hour later.

**What to change:** add one or two questions the person has to answer themselves, such as a budget range or when they want to buy. Fewer people will finish the form. The ones who do are worth calling.

### Did you ask for cheap leads?

The platform finds whoever is most likely to do the thing you asked for. Ask it for leads at the lowest cost and it finds people who fill in forms easily. Those are not the same people who buy.

**What to change:** stop judging a campaign by cost per lead and judge it by cost per sale. A campaign with dearer leads that brings customers beats one with cheap leads that brings none. The sum is in [LINK: what a lead should cost in Kenya -> /guides/cost-per-lead-kenya].

### Do the ad and the follow-up say the same thing?

The ad says "pay in instalments". The first WhatsApp message asks for the full deposit. The buyer feels misled and goes quiet.

**What to change:** read your ad, then read your first reply and your page as if you were the customer. The promise in the ad should be the first thing they see next.

### How fast do you reply?

Someone who inquires is interested now. By tomorrow they have asked three other businesses, or forgotten.

**What to change:** reply within minutes during working hours, and set an automatic first reply for the rest. The questions to ask in that first chat are in [LINK: how to qualify leads on WhatsApp -> /guides/qualify-leads-on-whatsapp].

### Does your ad put anyone off?

An ad that appeals to everybody attracts everybody, including people who could never afford you.

**What to change:** say who it is for and give a price range in the ad itself. Some people will scroll past, and that is what you want.

### What should I check this week?

Count last month's leads and last month's sales from ads. If you cannot say how many leads became customers, fix that first. If you can and the number is small, start with the first two changes above.

## faq
### Short answers
#### Should I spend more to get better leads?
Not yet. More budget on the same ad and the same form buys more of the same people. Change who the ad speaks to and what the form asks first.
#### Are Facebook leads worse than Google leads?
Not by nature. A person filling in a Facebook form was scrolling, so the follow-up has more work to do. Fast replies and one sorting question close most of the gap.
#### How many leads should become customers?
There is no single right number. Count your own for a month, then watch whether it moves after each change.
