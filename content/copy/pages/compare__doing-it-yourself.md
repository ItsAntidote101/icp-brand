## steps
### Five things to do this week
#### Write three numbers every Friday
Ad spend, inquiries, buyers, on one line in a notebook. Likes do not go on the line.
[LINK: How to know if your ads are working -> /guides/how-to-know-if-ads-are-working]
#### Time your own reply
Ask a friend to message your business at 9pm and note when the answer comes. Then read that answer the way a buyer would.
#### Answer the price with a question
"How much?" gets the price and one question back, such as when they need it. A price sent alone ends the chat.
[LINK: Why people ask for last price and disappear -> /guides/last-price-and-disappear]
#### Block the searches that never buy
In Google Ads, open the search terms report and add negative keywords: jobs, free, courses, towns you do not serve. Add each plural too, because Google does not cover it for you.
#### Ask Meta for inquiries
Set up your next ad in Ads Manager and pick the objective Meta calls "Leads", in place of boosting a post for likes.
[LINK: Boost post or Ads Manager -> /guides/boost-post-or-ads-manager]

## split
### Where it tends to stall
- The Friday numbers stop in the first busy week.
- You find a long list of words to block and no quick way to add them.
- Sales land on M-Pesa and the ads never hear about it.
- Nobody tells you whether what you pay per inquiry is fine or a problem.
