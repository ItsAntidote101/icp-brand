# From 2 deposits a month to 5 in three months
A property agency in Kilimani, on the same KES 120,000 a month. Inquiries went down, and that was the point.
[BUTTON: Show me where mine are lost -> /questionnaire]
[IMAGE: An agent opening the door of a show apartment in Kilimani for two visitors | 4:3]

## split
### Where they were in March
240 inquiries a month and a phone that never went quiet. "Is this still available?" "How much?" An agent sent the price and nothing came back. On Saturdays the van waited for eleven people who had confirmed a site visit on WhatsApp. Three showed up. Two deposits a month paid for all of it.
[IMAGE: A phone showing a WhatsApp chat that ends at the price of a two bedroom apartment | 3:4]

## steps
### What changed
#### They answered ten questions
The free diagnosis pointed at the gap between "How much?" and the site visit. The budget stayed where it was.
#### The ad said the price
New ad text from the fix plan put the starting price in the first line. People who could not reach it stopped asking.
#### The first reply asked something back
Agents pasted a reply that gives the price, then asks when the buyer wants to move. A second pasted message confirms each site visit the evening before.
#### Google stopped paying for renters
A file of words to block went into Google Ads in one upload: rentals, bedsitters, jobs.

## split
### What took longer than hoped
April looked worse. Inquiries dropped in the first weeks and deposits had not moved yet, so the owner nearly switched the old ads back on. Two agents kept sending the bare price for a fortnight. Saturday no-shows fell slowly and never reached zero. Each inquiry now costs KES 300 more, and nobody minds.
