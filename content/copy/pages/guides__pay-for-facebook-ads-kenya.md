# How do I pay for Facebook ads in Kenya?
By Visa or Mastercard, or by M-Pesa if your ad account is set to Kenya, runs in shillings, and had M-Pesa chosen when it was first set up. That last condition is the one that catches people.

## prose
### What does Meta accept from a Kenyan advertiser?
Meta's help page for Kenya lists three ways to pay:
- Credit cards and debit cards that carry the Visa or Mastercard mark, plus American Express in the currencies Amex supports.
- PayPal, in the currencies PayPal supports.
- M-Pesa, which Meta calls a local manual payment method.

### Why can I not see M-Pesa in my account?
Meta sets two conditions, and both must hold. The ad account's country must be Kenya with the currency set to Kenyan shillings. And a manual payment method must have been picked when the account was first set up.

If the first ad was paid by card, the account is on automatic billing, and Meta says a manual method can only be used where it was chosen at the start. That is why M-Pesa is missing from an account somebody opened in dollars with a card.

To see what you have, open Ads Manager, go to Billing & payments, then Payment settings. An account on M-Pesa shows a section called Available funds. An account on a card shows a current balance and when you will pay.

### How does paying by M-Pesa work?
It is prepaid. You add money first, the ads run, and Meta deducts from the balance up to once a day. When the balance is finished, the ads stop. Meta says the funds do not expire.

To add money, go to Payment settings, find Available funds and select Add funds. Enter the amount, choose the payment method, review and confirm. There is a minimum, and Meta shows it if you go under.

One caution for iPhone owners. Money added through the Facebook or Instagram apps on iOS is charged through Apple's App Store, and Apple keeps a service fee, which Meta puts at 30% in most regions. On KES 10,000 that is KES 3,000. Add funds from a web browser and the fee does not apply.

### How does paying by card work?
It is automatic, and you pay after the ads run. Meta charges the card each time your spend reaches an amount called the payment threshold, and again on your monthly bill date for whatever is left.

The threshold starts small and rises as payments go through, so several charges in one month is normal.

If the account's currency differs from your card's, each charge is converted. Meta says it adds no fee of its own for that, but your bank may. Ask the bank before the first charge, not after the fifth.

### Is VAT added on top?
Yes. Since November 2022 Meta has added Kenyan VAT to ad charges for every advertiser whose "sold to" country is Kenya, whether the ads are for business or personal use. Meta gives the rate only as the applicable local rate.

On a card, VAT is added on top of each charge. On M-Pesa, it is applied at the moment you add funds, so the amount you send and the amount left for ads are two different figures. Read the breakdown on the confirm screen. A KES 20,000 budget that forgets the tax buys less than KES 20,000 of ads.

If you are registered for VAT, add your VAT ID in payment settings so it prints on your receipts. Whether you can claim it back is a question for your accountant.

### Can I switch between M-Pesa and card later?
Not freely. This is what Meta's pages say:
- An account set up for available funds cannot move to automatic billing. You can still top up the available funds with a credit or debit card.
- An account set up for automatic billing does not get M-Pesa added.
- You can change an account's currency once every 60 days if no balance is owing, but doing so closes the old ad account, stops its ads and opens a new one.

Meta does not spell out whether that new account will offer M-Pesa, so I will not promise it. Settle country, currency and payment method before the first ad, as part of planning the launch.

## faq
### Questions owners ask next
#### Does Meta charge a fee when I add funds by M-Pesa?
Meta says it charges no provider fee for adding funds. Your payment provider might charge its own.
#### Can I get unused funds back?
Do not count on it. Meta says unused funds are generally not refundable. You can ask for a refund after closing the ad account, and Meta decides each request case by case.
#### How much should I add the first time?
I will not give you a number, because it depends on what a buyer is worth to you. Add only what you have planned to spend.
