# Why do people ask for last price and disappear?
A price sent alone ends the conversation, because it leaves them nothing to answer. Reply with the price and one question.
[IMAGE: A phone showing a WhatsApp chat where the business sent only a price and the message has two blue ticks and no reply | 16:9]

## prose
### Why do they go quiet after the price?

Read the chat from their side. They asked "How much?" You sent "KES 3,500". The exchange is finished. The only thing left for them to say is yes, and few people say yes to a bare number from a business they met five minutes ago.

"Last price" is not always haggling either. For something a person has never bought before, price is the one question they know how to ask. They do not know to ask what is included, how long it takes or how to pay.

Some of them were never going to buy. No wording changes that. The reply only has to make the next step easy for the ones who might.

### What should the reply contain?

Three parts, in one message.

- The price, or an honest range. Do not hide it.
- One thing the price includes, so the number means something.
- One question that is easy to answer and moves things forward.

The question does the work. "Which day suits you?" takes one word to answer. "Let me know" asks for nothing, so nothing comes back.

### What can I paste into WhatsApp today?

Swap in your own service, prices and days. The figures below are placeholders. Keep the shape.

When they ask "How much?":

"Hi [name], thanks for asking. The deep cleanse facial is KES 3,500. It takes about an hour and the products are included. I have Thursday 2pm or Saturday 10am open. Which one suits you?"

When they ask "Last price?" and you have room to move:

"The best I can do is KES 3,200 if you book today with a KES 1,000 deposit by M-Pesa. Shall I hold Thursday 2pm for you?"

When they ask "Last price?" and you have none:

"KES 3,500 is the price, and I would rather tell you now than waste your time. If your budget is closer to KES 2,000, the express facial is KES 1,800 and takes 30 minutes. Would that suit you better?"

When the price is large, such as a plot:

"Plots in this phase are KES 1.2M for an eighth of an acre. You can pay over 6 months after a deposit of KES 300,000. There is a site visit this Saturday at 9am, leaving from town. Shall I save you a seat?"

When they went quiet, the next day:

"Hi [name], the Saturday 10am slot is still open and I can hold it for you until 5pm today. Should I, or is another week better for you?"

If you take bookings, the deposit line matters as much as the price line. The clinic or salon version of this problem, with no-shows included, is on [LINK: the clinics and beauty page -> /for/clinics].

### How do I send these fast enough?

Save them once. In the WhatsApp Business app, open Quick replies, tap Add, paste the message, give it a shortcut such as "price" and save. In any chat, type "/" and your saved replies appear. Pick one, change the name and the day, and send. The app stores up to 50.

Whoever holds the phone on Saturday now answers the way you would.

### How many times should I follow up?

More than once, and each time with something new: an open slot, a photo or a date that is filling up. The pattern to use, and when to stop, is in [LINK: how to qualify inquiries on WhatsApp -> /guides/qualify-leads-on-whatsapp].

### How do I know the new wording is working?

Count before you change anything. Scroll back through last week's chats and note two numbers: how many times you sent a price, and how many of those got any reply afterwards. Count again after a week of the new wording.

Say it was 5 replies from 30 prices, and it becomes 11 from 30. That is your own evidence, and it beats anybody's opinion, ours included. If the number does not move, the leak is somewhere else, and you found that out in a week.

## faq
### Short answers
#### Should I hold back the price until they answer my questions?
No. Give the price or a range first, then ask your question. Hiding it keeps the wrong people in the chat and irritates the right ones.
#### Is everyone who asks for last price wasting my time?
No. Some are comparing and some do not know what else to ask. One good reply sorts them faster than guessing does.
#### What if they still go quiet?
Follow up the next day with something new, such as an open slot. After a few tries, label the chat and move on.
