# A media buyer for Kenyan businesses that pay for ads.
Ideal ICP is a media buying service based in Nairobi. It exists because good businesses keep paying for inquiries that never buy, and somebody should find out why.

## split
### The same story in every account
Money goes out every month, inquiries come in, and few become sales. The owner blames the agency, the agency blames the budget. In the accounts our founder opened, it was almost never the budget. The wrong people were asking, or a serious buyer got the same slow reply as everyone else.
[IMAGE: A media buyer pointing at a row in a client's spreadsheet on a laptop, phone face up beside it | 4:3]

## split
### What we believe
- Do not put another shilling into ads until you know why the inquiries you already paid for are not buying.
- You should get the work: files to upload and words to paste.
- A media buyer who stays with you is worth more than any score.
- If your numbers are healthy, you should hear it.
[IMAGE: A media buyer on a call at a desk, notebook open, a WhatsApp chat open on the phone | 3:4]

## split
### Why the name
ICP stands for ideal customer profile: a clear picture of the person most likely to buy from you. The diagnosis checks how far your ads and your replies are from that person.
[IMAGE: A notebook page describing one real buyer: where they live, their budget, how they first ask | 4:3]

## ladder
### A media buyer and software
You should know which is which.
#### What your media buyer does
Takes the free call, reads the numbers you log, writes your monthly note and answers your messages.
#### What the software does
Asks the questions, drafts your report from your answers and researches your market every week. The software is labelled as AI and shows its sources.
#### What neither does
Log in to your ad accounts, or dress an estimate up as a fact.

## cards
### What we will not do
#### Call you unless you ask
One free 20 minute call if you want it. If you do not ask, your phone stays quiet.
#### Ask for your ad account login
The diagnosis runs on ten answers. Rough numbers from memory are enough.
#### Run your ads for you unasked
Ideal ICP does not run your ads. For full management, ask us by message.
#### Promise you a result
We say what your media buyer does and how often. What your buyers do is up to them.
#### Tie you to a contract
You pay for one month by M-Pesa or card. Nothing renews on its own.
