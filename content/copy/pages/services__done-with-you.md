## faq
### Questions about sessions
#### Do I have to give you my password?
No. We work in your own accounts with you logged in. I never ask for a login.
#### Does an unused session carry over?
No. Sessions are counted by calendar month, and a new one opens on the 1st.
#### Can I book a session without a plan?
No. Sessions need Pro or Agency. The diagnosis and one 20 minute call are free.
#### What if my job is not on the list?
Choose "Something else" when you ask and tell me what you need.

## notes
"We" on this page means Eugene and the owner in a session together. It is never a team.
The jobs are the four in src/lib/hands-on.ts: connect my CRM or lead sheet, fix my conversion tracking, set up or restructure my campaigns, something else. The request form in src/app/dashboard/handson.tsx asks for the job, a WhatsApp number and a preferred time.
Limits come from the brief and the code: one a month on Pro, up to four on Agency, none on Free or Starter.
"Does an unused session carry over?" is answered from the code: sessions are counted per calendar month in Nairobi time and the dashboard says a new one opens on the 1st. This is a policy statement. Confirm Eugene wants it said this plainly.
"Do I have to give you my password?" follows the brief (no access to your ad account) and the About page (Eugene does not log in to ad accounts). How Eugene sees the owner's screen in a session is not stated anywhere I could read. Confirm.
Session length and format (call, video, in person) are not stated in the brief or the code, so the page gives neither. The two images assume a call. Change them if sessions are held another way.
"You see every step" describes the session. It does not promise the owner can do it alone afterwards.
