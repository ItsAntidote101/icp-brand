## faq
### Money words
#### CPL (cost per lead)
What one inquiry cost you: spend divided by inquiries. KES 20,000 for 40 inquiries is KES 500 each.
#### CPC (cost per click)
What one click on your ad cost. KES 3,000 for 100 clicks is KES 30 a click.
#### CPM
The cost of showing your ad 1,000 times. KES 2,000 for 10,000 showings is a CPM of KES 200.
#### CTR (click-through rate)
Clicks divided by the number of times the ad was shown. Meta reports two: "CTR (all)" counts any click on the ad, and "CTR (link)" counts only clicks through to your site or chat.
#### ROAS (return on ad spend)
Sales value divided by ad spend. KES 150,000 in sales from KES 50,000 of ads is 3. That is revenue, so your costs still have to come out of it.
#### Impressions
How many times your ad was shown, repeats included.
#### Reach
How many different people saw your ad at least once.
#### Frequency
Impressions divided by reach: the average number of times each person saw the ad. 30,000 impressions to 10,000 people is a frequency of 3.

## faq
### Meta words
#### Ad that clicks to WhatsApp
An ad whose button opens a chat with your business number. It needs a WhatsApp Business number connected to your Facebook Page.
#### Instant form
A form that opens inside Facebook or Instagram with the person's details already filled in. The "Higher intent" type adds a step where they confirm before sending.
#### Customer list custom audience
Your own customer list, uploaded so Meta can show those people ads or leave them out. Meta matches it in scrambled form, and the list may not hold health or financial information.
#### Lookalike audience
New people Meta picks because they resemble a list you gave it. The source list needs at least 100 people, and Meta limits the option for some finance and housing ads.
#### Advantage+ campaign budget
One budget for the whole Meta campaign, which Meta moves between your ad sets. The other choice is a separate budget on each ad set.
#### Learning phase
The stretch after a new ad set or a big edit, when results swing. Meta says it usually ends after about 50 results in a week.

## faq
### Google words
#### Negative keyword
A word or phrase that stops your Google ad from showing. Add "jobs" and you stop paying for job seekers. Plurals and similar words are not covered, so add "job" as well.
#### Search terms report
The list of what people really typed before your ad showed. Keywords are what you chose, and search terms are what you got.
#### Match type
How loosely Google matches your keyword to what people type. Broad is the default and the loosest, phrase is tighter and exact is the tightest.
#### Quality Score
Google's 1 to 10 rating of a keyword's ad and landing page against other advertisers. Treat it as a warning light, since it is not a charge on your bill.
#### Manual CPC
A bidding setting where you decide the most you will pay for a click.
#### Maximize conversions
A bidding setting where Google sets the bids and spends your whole budget on as many conversions as it can get, with no limit on what each one costs.
#### Target CPA
A bidding setting where you name the cost per conversion you want, say KES 800, and Google bids towards it. Some accounts show it as "Maximize conversions with a Target CPA", which behaves the same.

## faq
### Tracking words
#### Conversion
The action you told the platform to count: a form, a call, a WhatsApp chat or a sale. If it was told to count forms, it has no idea who paid.
#### Meta pixel
Code on your website that tells Meta what visitors did after the ad.
#### Conversions API
A way to send the same news to Meta straight from your own records, sales made offline included.
#### Offline conversion
A sale made away from the website, by M-Pesa, cash or a signed agreement, and reported to the ad platform afterwards.
