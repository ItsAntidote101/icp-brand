## cards
### Where a serious buyer drops off
Three places a media buyer looks before touching the ad budget.
#### The price goes out alone
They ask "How much?" and get a figure with no question back. From their side the conversation is finished. From yours, they ghosted after the price.
#### Nobody confirms the site visit
A yes on Wednesday is not a seat on Saturday. With no message the evening before, you are fuelling a van for joyriders.
#### They cannot tell you are genuine
Someone who filled a form from abroad wants to read "title deed ready", and how to check it, before they agree to a visit.

## split
### What the diagnosis checks for a property seller
Ten questions, about 3 minutes. Rough numbers from memory will do. It asks who inquires and how fast they hear back, then how many pay. You get back:
- Your cost per inquiry and cost per sale
- What one more buyer in every ten inquiries is worth
- A score out of 100, your top problems and 3 fixes for this week

## faq
### Questions property sellers ask
#### Can you get rid of the joyriders?
Not every one. The diagnosis shows whether the wrong people are your main problem, or whether you are losing good buyers after they ask. Each needs a different fix.
#### Does it change anything if most buyers are abroad?
The questions stay the same, but the reply changes. A form filled overnight from abroad needs an answer waiting in the morning, and proof the buyer can check from where they are.
#### What if the numbers turn out fine?
Then the report says so and shows what to protect before you spend more. Better you hear that from us than pay for a fix you do not need.
#### What does it cost after the free part?
Starter is KES 6,500 a month, paid by M-Pesa or card. Nothing renews on its own and there is no contract.
