# How do I know if my ads are working?
Put spend, inquiries and buyers on one line each week. If the buyers column is empty you do not know yet, and likes do not belong on the line at all.
[IMAGE: A notebook page with four weekly lines of spend, inquiries and buyers, next to a phone showing M-Pesa payment messages | 16:9]

## prose
### What goes on the line?

Three numbers, written down every Monday for the week before.

- Spend. What the ads cost you that week, in shillings, as shown in your Google or Meta account.
- Inquiries. Every call, WhatsApp chat and form that came from the ads.
- Buyers. The people among those inquiries who paid.

A notebook is enough. So is a spreadsheet with four columns: week, spend, inquiries, buyers.

Then do two sums. Divide spend by inquiries to see what one inquiry cost. Do the same with buyers. Say a week reads KES 15,000 spent, 30 inquiries, 2 buyers. Each inquiry cost KES 500 and each buyer KES 7,500. Whether KES 7,500 is fine depends on what you keep from one sale, and that sum is in [LINK: the guide on cost per lead in Kenya -> /guides/cost-per-lead-kenya].

### Why do the ad reports look good when sales do not?

Each platform reports what it was asked to get. In Meta Ads Manager, the Results column counts the outcome the campaign was set up for. A boosted post set up for engagement reports engagement. An ad set up for messages reports "messaging conversations started". Neither column knows who paid.

Google works the same way. A conversion there is whatever action the account was told to count, such as a form or a call. The payment on M-Pesa or across your desk happens where neither platform can see it.

So a report can look healthy while the till is quiet. Two hundred likes and no deposit is a report doing exactly what it was asked.

### How do I count buyers who pay by M-Pesa or in person?

You count them yourself. Three habits make it easy.

- Ask every new inquiry "Where did you see us?" and write the answer beside their name.
- Keep ad inquiries apart. In the WhatsApp Business app, give chats that came from ads their own label.
- When someone pays, go back to the list and mark them as a buyer, with the date and the amount.

Later, you can send the same list back to the platforms so they learn what a buyer looks like. Meta takes it as a file uploaded in Events Manager or through the Conversions API. Google calls it offline conversion imports and now recommends a route named enhanced conversions for leads. That is a setup job for another day. The notebook comes first.

### How long should I wait before I judge?

Longer than a few days. A new Meta ad set starts in what Meta calls the learning phase, when results swing. Meta says it usually ends after about 50 results in the week after the last significant edit. An ad set that cannot get enough results is marked "Learning limited".

Then there is your own selling time. A salon booking can close the same afternoon. A plot can take weeks from the first "Is this still available?" to the deposit. So put each buyer on the line for the week their inquiry arrived, even if they paid later. Old lines will fill in as time passes. Compare at least four of them before you decide anything.

### What does working look like?

One buyer costs you less than you keep from the sale, with room to spare, and that holds for several weeks running. That is the whole test.

If your line passes it, the ads are working and we will say so. If inquiries are steady and buyers are thin, the ads may be fine and the leak sits after the inquiry: who is asking, how fast they get a reply, whether anyone follows up. Putting more money in will not fix that part. On a paid plan, the Ideal ICP dashboard tracks your cost per lead every week, so it keeps the first half of the line for you. See [LINK: how the dashboard works -> /how-it-works].

### What should I stop counting?

Likes, reach, impressions and click rates tell you whether people saw the ad. They are worth a look when something breaks, but they stay off the line because none of them is money.

## faq
### Short answers
#### Do likes and followers mean my ads are working?
No. They show that people saw the ad. Only inquiries and buyers show it was worth paying for.
#### What if I cannot tell which buyers came from ads?
Start asking every new inquiry where they saw you, from today. After four weeks you have four lines to compare.
#### Do I need tracking set up on Google or Meta first?
No. A notebook and the question "Where did you see us?" come first. Tracking makes the count faster later.
