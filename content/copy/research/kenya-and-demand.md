Compiled 24 Nov 2026. Help pages carry no publish date, so they are marked "read 24 Nov 2026". Use a number only with its source and where it was measured.

## Part 1. Kenyan context, sourced

### Phones, WhatsApp, M-Pesa

- At 31 Mar 2026: 84.09 million SIM subscriptions, 62.6 million mobile data subscriptions, 50.2 million smartphones, 53.37 million mobile money subscriptions, 602,470 mobile money agents. [CA sector statistics Q3 FY2025/26, Jun 2026](https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-06/Sector%20Statistics%20Report%20Quarter%203%20%20FY%202025-2026.pdf)
- 57% of people surveyed had used the internet in the past 7 days (Apr to Jun 2026). Facebook and WhatsApp are the two most used platforms. Audience and ad activity peak from 7pm to 10pm. [CA Audience Measurement report, Aug 2026](https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-08/%20Audience%20Measurement%20and%20Industry%20Trends%20Report%20Quarter%204%202025-2026.pdf)
- 72.5% of internet users aged 16+ said they used WhatsApp in the past month (GWI, Q2 2026). Facebook ads can reach 19.0 million people in Kenya. [DataReportal Digital 2027 Kenya, 5 Nov 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-kenya). Caution: DataReportal counts only 23.9 million internet users (40.6%), well below CA's measures. Never mix the two.
- 52.6% of adults used mobile money daily in 2024, up from 23.6% in 2021. [FinAccess 2024 (CBK, KNBS, FSD Kenya), 3 Dec 2024](https://www.fsdkenya.org/blogs-publications/2024-finaccess-household-survey-key-insights-into-kenyas-financial-landscape/)
- Year to Mar 2026: M-PESA moved KES 41.68 trillion in 46.4 billion transactions; about 1.0 million Lipa na M-PESA merchants and 2.1 million Pochi la Biashara tills. Safaricom results via [tech-ish, 7 May 2026](https://tech-ish.com/2026/05/07/safaricom-fy26-mpesa-results-explained/)
- Meta takes M-PESA for ads in Kenya, but only on a KES ad account that chose it at setup. VAT is added to Meta ad charges in Kenya. [Meta help: Kenya payments](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1946853292237629), [Meta help: Kenya VAT](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/187911129790766), read 24 Nov 2026
- Insurance premiums equalled 2.63% of GDP in 2025. [The Standard citing IRA, 3 Sep 2026](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/business/article/2001556825/insurance-premiums-cross-sh466b-mark-as-penetration-grows-to-2-6pc)

### Platform names, confirmed 24 Nov 2026

Google
- **Keyword upload in Google Ads Editor.** "Make multiple changes" (paste from a spreadsheet) or Account > Import > From file (CSV). Rows arrive as proposed changes to keep or reject. [Add multiple keywords](https://support.google.com/google-ads/editor/answer/56501?hl=en), [Import a CSV file](https://support.google.com/google-ads/editor/answer/30564?hl=en)
- **Negative keywords.** Name unchanged. They do not cover synonyms or close variants, so plurals must be added one by one. [About negative keywords](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972?hl=en), [Editor: add negative keywords](https://support.google.com/google-ads/editor/answer/30553?hl=en)
- **Bidding.** "Manual CPC", "Maximize conversions", "Target CPA". Google says that from June 2026 Target CPA shows as its own option again; some accounts still see "Maximize conversions with a Target CPA". Same behaviour. [Bid strategies](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2472725?hl=en), [label change](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10353027?hl=en)
- **Sales that happen offline.** "Offline conversion imports". Google now recommends "enhanced conversions for leads" through "Data Manager"; file uploads are marked legacy. [About offline conversion imports](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2998031?hl=en)

Meta
- **Customer list custom audience.** CSV or TXT. Phone numbers need the country code. The list is hashed before matching. No health or financial information allowed, which matters for clinics, lenders and insurers. [Create](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/170456843145568), [prepare data](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/606443329504150)
- **Offline sales.** The old Offline Conversions API ended in May 2025. Sales now go to a "dataset" by CSV upload in Events Manager or through the "Conversions API". [Upload offline event data](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/155437961572700)
- **Budget.** The Help Center says "Advantage+ campaign budget", not "Advantage campaign budget" (developer docs still use the older form). One budget shared across ad sets. The alternative is "ad set budgets". On by default for sales, leads and app campaigns. [About campaign and ad set budgets](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/458847204894307), [developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/bidding/guides/advantage-campaign-budget)
- **Lead forms.** "Instant forms", with types "More volume" (default), "Higher intent" (adds a review screen; mobile Facebook and Instagram feed only) and "Rich creative". [About instant form types](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/252352181957512)
- **Lead quality goal.** Now "Maximize number of qualified leads" (older pages say "conversion leads"). From April 2026 new campaigns need a Conversions API link to use it. [Performance goals for lead ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/782657799338685)
- **WhatsApp ads.** Official name "ads that click to WhatsApp". Needs a WhatsApp Business number connected to the Page. [Create ads that click to WhatsApp](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/447934475640650)

## Part 2. How owners in five trades talk about it

Reasoning from general knowledge of how these businesses run in Kenya. Unsourced unless a link follows. Quotes are typical phrasing, not recorded speech.

**Property and real estate**
- Inquiry: "Is this still available?", "How much?", "Send location", a form filled from abroad.
- Sale: site visit, then booking fee or deposit, then sale agreement.
- How lost: price sent with no question back; site visit never confirmed; no proof the seller is genuine. A Nairobi agent told the Nation that scams make trust hard: [Nation, date not captured](https://nation.africa/kenya/life-and-style/realtors-lean-on-tiktok-youtube-to-grow-business--4510164)
- Words: "joyriders", "serious buyers only", "they ghost after the price", "site visit", "title deed ready".
- Scene: Saturday, 9am, the van idles at the stage. Eleven confirmed on WhatsApp. Three show up.

**Schools and colleges**
- Inquiry: "Fee structure?", "Do you have transport from Syokimau?", "When is the next intake?"
- Sale: school tour or interview, admission letter, first term fees paid.
- How lost: fee structure PDF sent and nothing after; evening inquiries wait for the office; nobody calls back before reporting day.
- Words: "intake", "admissions", "fee structure", "parents are just window shopping", "the bursar".
- Scene: a parent messages at 9pm in January. The reply comes Tuesday. She has paid another school.

**Insurance and financial services**
- Inquiry: "How much is comprehensive for a 2015 Axio?", a quote form, "Do you cover maternity?"
- Sale: premium paid and policy issued; for lenders, a funded loan.
- How lost: quote sent as a PDF with no call; documents never chased; renewals forgotten.
- Words: "quotation", "they went quiet after the quote", "tire kickers", "renewals", "premium".
- Scene: forty quotes sent this month, each a PDF, each ending "Kindly advise".

**Clinics and beauty**
- Inquiry: "How much is braces?", "Bei ya facial?", "Are you open Sunday?"
- Sale: a booking that shows up; better, a deposit paid by M-Pesa.
- How lost: a price list sent with no slot offered; no deposit so no-shows; front desk too busy to answer WhatsApp.
- Words: "no-shows", "walk-ins", "price shoppers", "bookings".
- Scene: 2pm slot, chair empty, the client's last message still reads "I'll come tomorrow".

**Professional services (law, accounting, consulting)**
- Inquiry: a long voice note about a land dispute; "How much to register a company?"
- Sale: paid consultation, then signed engagement letter and retainer.
- How lost: free advice given in the chat; fee note never followed up; no intake questions.
- Words: "consultation fee", "retainer", "fee note", "brief", "they wanted free advice".
- Scene: forty minutes explaining succession law by phone to someone who then asks for "a small discount".
- Caution: advocates face advertising limits, such as no promised outcomes (reported 2016; check current rules). [Nairobi Law Monthly, 1 Sep 2016](https://nairobilawmonthly.com/what-is-the-point-in-restricting-advertising-for-legal-services/)

### 20 article topics

Links name the page or service; no slugs exist yet.

1. **Why are my Google ads not converting?** ("why are my google ads not converting") Usually the clicks come from searches you never meant to pay for. Link: Google keyword pack.
2. **Boost post or Ads Manager: which one?** ("boost post vs ads manager") A boost buys attention; Ads Manager lets you ask for inquiries and say who should see the ad. Link: campaign setup plan.
3. **How do I pay for Facebook ads in Kenya?** (exact) By card, or by M-PESA if the account was opened in shillings with M-PESA chosen at setup. Link: campaign setup plan.
4. **Google ads or Facebook ads: which is better?** (exact) Google catches people already searching and Meta interrupts people who were not, so ask whether your buyers search. Link: free diagnosis.
5. **Why am I getting fake leads from Facebook ads?** ("fake leads from facebook ads") An easy form invites careless taps, so add a question and a review step. Link: reply wording pack.
6. **Why is my cost per lead so high?** (exact) A high cost per inquiry matters less than what each buyer costs, so count buyers first. Link: cost per lead dashboard.
7. **How long do Facebook ads take to work?** (exact) Meta needs about a week of steady results before delivery settles. Link: free diagnosis.
8. **How do I know if my ads are working?** ("how to know if your ads are working") Put spend, inquiries and buyers on one line each week; likes do not belong on it. Link: cost per lead dashboard.
9. **Click to WhatsApp ads or lead forms?** ("click to whatsapp ads", "meta ads leads form") WhatsApp brings faster chats, forms bring details to call back, and both fail without a reply plan. Link: reply wording pack.
10. **Meta ads: leads, sales, engagement or traffic?** ("meta ads leads vs engagement") Meta finds the people most likely to do what you asked for, so ask for inquiries, not likes. Link: campaign setup plan.
11. **Which negative keywords should I add?** ("negative keywords list") Block the searches that cost money and never buy: jobs, free, courses, places you do not serve. Link: Google keyword pack.
12. **Should I hire a digital marketing agency?** (exact) First find out why the inquiries you already paid for did not buy. Link: pricing.
13. **How do I track sales from Facebook ads?** (exact) If the sale happens on M-Pesa or in person, you have to tell Meta, from your own spreadsheet. Link: Meta customer files; hands-on session.
14. **How often should I follow up with leads?** ("how often to follow up with leads") More than once, on a schedule, with something new to say each time. Link: reply wording pack.
15. **Why do people ask for last price and disappear?** ("why do people ask for last price") A price sent alone ends the conversation, so answer with the price and a question. Link: clinics and beauty; property.
16. **How do I sell land fast in Kenya using ads?** ("how to sell land fast in kenya") Fast sales come from fewer, checked inquiries and a confirmed site visit, not a bigger budget. Link: property.
17. **How do I get more admissions in my school?** ("how to get more admission in school") Many schools lose parents between the fee structure and the tour, so check that gap before the next intake. Link: schools and colleges.
18. **How do I advertise insurance on Facebook?** (exact) Ask one question that sorts people before the quote, because Meta limits some targeting for financial products. Link: insurance and financial services.
19. **How do I get more patients in my clinic?** ("how to get more patients in your clinic") Count how many inquiries became bookings that showed up before buying more inquiries. Link: clinics and beauty.
20. **How do law firms get clients from ads?** ("how do law firms get clients") Search ads reach people with a live problem; the work is sorting who will pay for a consultation. Link: professional services.

### 25 glossary terms

Checked against the Part 1 help pages plus: Meta [CTR (link)](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/877711998984611), [CPM](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/753932008002620), [reach and frequency](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/230299314945919), [purchase ROAS](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/274294333328345), [learning phase](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700), [lookalike audience](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/465262276878947); Google [Quality Score](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167118?hl=en), [search terms report](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2472708?hl=en), [match types](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7478529?hl=en-GB). Read 24 Nov 2026. Shilling examples are arithmetic, not benchmarks.

1. **CPL (cost per lead).** Spend divided by inquiries. KES 20,000 for 40 inquiries is KES 500 each.
2. **CPC (cost per click).** What one click costs. KES 3,000 for 100 clicks is KES 30.
3. **CPM.** The cost of showing your ad 1,000 times.
4. **CTR (click-through rate).** Clicks divided by times shown. Meta reports two: "CTR (all)" counts any click, "CTR (link)" only clicks to your site or chat.
5. **ROAS (return on ad spend).** Sales value divided by ad spend. KES 150,000 in sales from KES 50,000 of ads is 3. Revenue, not profit.
6. **Conversion.** The action you told the platform to count: a form, a call, a WhatsApp chat, a sale.
7. **Impressions.** How many times the ad was shown, repeats included.
8. **Reach.** How many different people saw it at least once.
9. **Frequency.** Impressions divided by reach: the average times each person saw your ad.
10. **Meta pixel.** Code on your website that tells Meta what visitors did after the ad.
11. **Conversions API.** The same news sent to Meta straight from your records, offline sales included.
12. **Customer list custom audience.** Your own customer list, matched by Meta in scrambled form so you can show them ads or leave them out.
13. **Lookalike audience.** New people Meta picks because they resemble your list. Needs a source of at least 100 people; limited for some finance and housing ads.
14. **Negative keyword.** A word that blocks your Google ad. Add "jobs" and you stop paying for job seekers.
15. **Search terms report.** What people really typed before your ad showed. Keywords are what you chose; search terms are what you got.
16. **Match type.** How loosely Google matches your keyword to searches. Broad is the default and the loosest; phrase is tighter; exact is tightest.
17. **Quality Score.** Google's 1 to 10 rating of a keyword's ad and landing page against other advertisers. A warning light, not a charge.
18. **Manual CPC.** You set the most you will pay for a click yourself.
19. **Maximize conversions.** Google sets the bids and spends your whole budget on as many conversions as it can, with no cost limit on each.
20. **Target CPA.** You name the cost per conversion you want, say KES 800, and Google bids towards it.
21. **Advantage+ campaign budget.** One Meta budget for the whole campaign, moved between ad sets by Meta.
22. **Learning phase.** The stretch after a new ad set or big edit, when results swing. Meta says it usually ends after about 50 results in a week.
23. **Instant form.** A form that opens inside Facebook or Instagram, details prefilled. The "Higher intent" type adds a confirm step.
24. **Ad that clicks to WhatsApp.** An ad whose button opens a chat with your business number.
25. **Offline conversion.** A sale made away from the website (M-Pesa, cash, signed agreement) and reported to the ad platform afterwards.
